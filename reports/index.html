Security remediation

all HIGH fixed · few deferred

A 40-agent audit (30 domain reviewers + 10 red-team "hackers") produced 104 raw findings; adversarial verification confirmed 33 (6 High, 10 Medium, 14 Low, + 3 controls that held under attack). The red-team's headline: owner-account takeover is not achievable — owner authority is env-only, no route mutates an account's email, and session/MFA tokens are unforgeable HMACs. All 6 High findings are fixed and live; the auth, hardening, and forensics batches are live; a handful of low-severity items are deferred by design (non-exploitable, documented, tracked).
